ABOUT

Easy enough for anyone

Write lines, choices, and characters with buttons and forms. Your story plays from the first line, even before any art exists.

Open enough for coders

The same project is plain text with a command-line tool and git-friendly files. Extend it with your own statements.

Light enough for any PC

A finished game is about 3 MB before your art and music, and it starts with a double-click on Windows 10 or 11.

FEATURES

FOR WRITERS

Each line of your story is a row.

Pick who speaks and how they look, then type what they say. Choices, music, and backgrounds come from menus and forms, never code.

  • The game updates as you type
  • Play from any line
  • Autosave and a history of every change
Screenplay
FOR ARTISTS

Make the menus look like your game.

Move, resize, and recolor the textbox, choices, and every menu right on the running game. Start from a preset and get a contrast check as you go.

  • Layered sprites with blinking and lip-flap
  • Import from Photoshop
  • Seven presets and color palettes
