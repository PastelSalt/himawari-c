# Credit Himawari (optional)

**You don't have to credit Himawari.** The engine inside your game is under the zlib license, which doesn't require any credit. It's appreciated, but it's your game and your call. Himawari is quiet by default, so nobody gets a message they didn't choose.

## What is always included
Every exported game contains a `CREDITS.txt` next to its `.exe`, with the notices for the libraries inside it. That file includes **one credit that is required**: FreeType, the library that draws the text, asks that documentation say it is used. `CREDITS.txt` already does this, so just **leave the file in the folder** when you share your game. That's all you have to do.

## If you'd like to credit Himawari too
Pick whichever feels right:
- **Do nothing.** `CREDITS.txt` already says the game was made with Himawari and links to the project.
- **Mention it in your game's own credits**, store page, or description: "Made with Himawari". There's no required wording.
- **Add a small "Made with Himawari" line on the title screen**, styled by your theme. In Studio, the **UI Designer** can place it, and the choice lives in your project settings. *(This option is coming in Studio before v1.0.)*
- **Put it in the window title**, for example "My Game (made with Himawari)". *(Also coming before v1.0.)*

When those options arrive, Studio will ask once, the first time you export a project, and remember your answer. You can change it later. The default will stay the quiet one: credits file only.

## Your own credits
Your game's art, music, and fonts keep their own licenses. If you use sounds or pictures from other people, thank them in your game's credits and check what their license asks for. The templates and examples that come with Himawari are CC0, so they never need a credit.
