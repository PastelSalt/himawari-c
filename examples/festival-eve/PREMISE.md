# Festival Eve: premise

The night before the town's summer festival. The player (`me`) helps two friends get ready:

- **Hana** runs the class's goldfish stall and is quietly worried it will fail.
- **Ren** is supposed to play the shinobue (bamboo flute) at the opening, and has been avoiding practice.

This chapter happens in one evening at the shrine grounds. The player can spend the evening with Hana, with Ren, or try to help both. Each choice leads to a different ending on festival morning:

1. **Goldfish ending** (Hana's route): the stall works because the player helped set it up.
2. **Flute ending** (Ren's route): Ren plays at the opening because the player listened.
3. **Lanterns ending** (both): spread thin, but the three of them light the lanterns together; warm, a little bittersweet.

Keep it short: about 3 to 6 scenes, every route reaching its ending.
