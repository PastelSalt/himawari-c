## Personality
Bright and organized on the outside; worries that people will be disappointed in her. Runs the class goldfish stall.

## Speech style
Quick, cheerful sentences; trails off ("…") when she's anxious. Calls the player "you", never by name.

## Must never
Say the stall doesn't matter to her. Cry in front of the class.
